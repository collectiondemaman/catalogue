The apartment

A home with a story

Step into a light-filled, peaceful 63 m² apartment decorated with carefully selected French vintage furniture and objects. Its open kitchen, comfortable living space and private balcony make it ideal for a city break, a family trip or a longer stay.

The entire apartment is yours, with two separate bedrooms, a bathroom, living room, open kitchen and balcony.

The essentials

Everything for an easy stay

Sleep comfortably

One 160 × 200 cm double bed and two 90 × 190 cm single beds. Ideal for up to four guests.

Cook and unwind

Equipped kitchen with oven, microwave, dishwasher, fridge/freezer, coffee machine and utensils.

Stay connected

High-speed fibre Wi-Fi, TV and a dedicated workspace.

Easy access

Metro Line 4 is approximately one minute on foot; a lift makes luggage handling easier.

Everyday convenience

Auchan supermarket downstairs and a swimming and sports centre a short walk away.

Travel at your pace

Self check-in, a secure building and convenient transport connections across Paris.

Explore Paris

Paris is just a metro ride away

Located in Bagneux, just south of Paris, the apartment is only steps from Metro Line 4, with direct connections towards central Paris and convenient transfers to other lines.

Allow approximately 20 minutes to the Notre-Dame area and around 30 minutes for many central Paris destinations, depending on your route and traffic conditions.
