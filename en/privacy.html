Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Collection de Maman handles information in connection with its online catalogue and Pinterest publishing integration.

Catalogue and favorites

The catalogue uses browser storage to remember your favorites on your device. You can clear this data through your browser settings. Our hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the catalogue.

Enquiries and orders

When you contact us, we use the details you share to answer your enquiry and, where applicable, arrange a purchase or delivery. Email and messaging services handle messages under their own privacy policies.

Optional website analytics

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand traffic sources and how people use the English catalogue. Google may use cookies to measure visits. You can change your choice at any time. See Google's privacy policy.

Pinterest publishing integration

We plan to use the Pinterest API to publish original catalogue Pins to our own business account and review performance metrics such as impressions, saves and outbound clicks. Pinterest handles activity on its platform under its privacy policy.
