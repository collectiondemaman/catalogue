The story behind Collection de Maman

Collection de Maman means “Mum’s Collection” in French. The name comes from a simple idea: every cherished object deserves to be loved again.

For me, vintage is more than decoration. Every table, chair, ceramic, mirror or lamp has lived a life before. These objects have witnessed family meals, celebrations, quiet mornings and everyday moments. Time may have left marks on them, but it has also given them character.

As a mother, I hope one day to pass my love for beautiful objects on to my daughter. Until then, I share this collection with people around the world who appreciate craftsmanship, history and the beauty of imperfect things.

Each piece is personally sourced in France. Most are one of a kind and selected not because they are perfect, but because they have a presence, a history and a story worth continuing.

When one of these pieces finds a new home, it becomes part of another family’s story.
